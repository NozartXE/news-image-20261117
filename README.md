front page: Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge again, tankers hit mines in the
Strait of Hormuz, Xi, Trump and Putin head for a first three-way summit in
Shenzhen, and Nvidia reels after Jensen Huang's exit — with the day's wire
briefs along the bottom. The picture is drawn programmatically by
`build_image.py` (Pillow, deterministic) and delivered by `main.py`.
